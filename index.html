❔ How does it work?

Play a round to set your score, then tap Challenge a friend — you get a link that holds your exact questions and score. Send it anywhere. When your friend opens it, they face the same questions and find out who won. Win duels to grow your 🔥 streak. After every question you'll see a quick explanation, so you learn while you play!

A free Science quiz game for students

Quiz Gyan is a free, fast and fun way to revise Science. Pick a difficulty, answer ten multiple-choice questions against the clock, and read a short explanation after each one so you actually learn. Then challenge a classmate to a quiz battle and see who comes out on top. It works on any phone or computer — no downloads, no sign-up.

Frequently asked questions

What is Quiz Gyan?

Quiz Gyan is a free online Science quiz game for students. You answer multiple-choice questions, get an instant explanation after each one, and can challenge your friends to quiz battles to see who scores higher.

Is Quiz Gyan free to use?

Yes, Quiz Gyan is completely free. There is no sign-up, no app to download and no payment — you just open the website and start playing in your browser.

What topics does the Science quiz cover?

The Science quiz covers physics, chemistry, biology, and Earth and space science. It has more than 250 questions split across Easy, Medium and Hard levels, with topics like the human body, plants, matter, energy, light, sound, the Solar System and famous scientists.

Who is Quiz Gyan for?

Quiz Gyan is made for school students and is especially suitable for Class 7 (Grade 7) and other middle-school learners. The questions follow general science taught at this level, so students anywhere in the world can use it.

How do I challenge a friend to a quiz battle?

Play a round to set your score, then tap 'Challenge a friend'. Quiz Gyan creates a link that holds your questions and your score. Send the link to a friend — when they open it, they answer the same questions and find out who won.

How many questions are in each quiz?

Each quiz has 10 questions by default, and you can also choose 5 or 7. Every time you retake the quiz you get a fresh set, and you cycle through all the questions before any of them repeat.

Does Quiz Gyan help you learn while you play?

Yes. After every question, Quiz Gyan shows a short explanation of the correct answer, so you understand the science instead of just guessing. That makes it useful for revision and exam practice.
